# Recruiter outreach

The second half of the engine. Finding a good job is half the work; the other half is making sure a person on the other side knows I applied, and why it fits.

## What it does

1. **Follows the target companies.** Every company I apply to, plus the best open roles in the radar. Recruiters can filter candidates who have shown interest in their company, and following the page counts.
2. **Finds the recruiters at each company.** It looks for people in Talent Acquisition, Recruiting and People at each target company, and keeps name, title, mutual connections and profile link.
3. **Checks who I already know.** Anyone already in my network gets a direct message instead of a connection request, so no invitation is wasted.
4. **Writes a note for each person.** A connection note under 300 characters, and a longer message to send once they accept. Each one names the role and the company and uses one proof from my track record that is relevant to that job.
5. **Tracks every contact** in a tab of the portal, next to the jobs: to contact → invitation sent → connected → message sent → replied. After 7 days with no answer, the card flags that it is time for a follow-up.

## Rules

- **I send everything myself.** The engine prepares; I open the profile, paste the note and click send. Connection requests and messages are never automated: that is what gets accounts restricted, and it is not how I want to show up.
- **5 to 10 a day**, starting with companies that have an open role that fits.
- **Offer before request.** The message leads with what I solve for the company (the role, one result), not with what I need. One small ask: "keep me in mind" or "15 minutes?".
- **Only true facts**, the same verified career base used for the cover letters. No inflated claims to sound bigger.
- **People's data stays private.** Names and profiles live only in my portal, never in this repository.
