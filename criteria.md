# Scoring criteria

Each job gets a score from 0 to 100. The score does not decide for me: it orders the queue so I look first at what has the best chance.

| Criterion | Points | What earns them |
|---|---|---|
| Function | 30 | Leading brand and marketing |
| Seniority | 20 | Head, manager, director |
| Location | 20 | Remote anywhere; hybrid in Belo Horizonte; relocation only to São Paulo |
| Requirements | 15 | The job's core requirements compared with my real track record |
| Sector and size | 10 | B2B tech and HR tech score higher |
| Freshness | 5 | How recently it was posted |

## Filtered out

Junior, mid-level and analyst roles; execution roles without team leadership; on-site or hybrid outside Belo Horizonte and São Paulo; jobs posted more than 60 days ago; duplicates of jobs already in the portal, including discarded ones.

## Central-gap rule

Some jobs are built around one thing: hands-on SEO/AEO, high-volume paid media, years of hands-on product marketing, leading a sales team. If I can't prove that thing with delivered work (managing someone who does it doesn't count), the job only stays when the rest of it fits at least 80%. Below that, it goes to "Discarded" with the gap and the estimated fit written down. I can always bring it back.

## Salary

Salary never eliminates a job on its own. A job with no salary is marked "not informed". When a salary is listed, anything below my reference is flagged.

## Learning from discards

When I discard a job I pick a reason (seniority, location, salary, sector, central gap...). The next morning's run reads those reasons and tightens the filter accordingly.
